# Thor D2 – changes

## 0.1.7 (2026-11-12)
- Swap in the deck: drop an item on a taken place and the two swap. This also works on an equipped slot (ring on ring, weapon on weapon): the worn item goes to where the new one came from, or to a free place in the inventory.
- Double tap moves an item over: to the open stash or cube and back, a potion into the belt, a worn item into the inventory.
- Sort the stash: the *Stash* tab has *Sort* (stash open in the game), also for the large 10x10 stash.
- Gold: *Gold in* and *Gold out* in the *Stash* tab put all gold into the stash or take out as much as the character may carry.
- Sell junk: with a shop open, *Sell junk* sells all plain weapons and armour (white or grey, no sockets, not ethereal) at once. It asks once before it sells.
- Points in the deck: with free stat points a *+* shows next to strength, dexterity, vitality and energy. With free skill points the *Skills* tab shows all skills of the class and *Spend a point*.
- Waypoints in the deck: while the waypoint menu is open in the game, the deck lists the waypoints by act. Tap one to travel.
- All of it uses the game's own commands, single player only.

## 0.1.6 (2026-11-11)
- Drag and drop in the deck: drag an item to its new place (inventory, belt, equipped slots, stash and cube when open in the game). The item follows your finger and lands where you let go. Tapping works as before. Uses the same commands of the game, single player only.

## 0.1.5 (2026-11-08)
- Character name: the keyboard is now bound properly to the name field. Before, it could stay on the screen it had last used, so typing reached nothing.
- Keyboard on the lower screen: with the Thor option that shows the keyboard on the second screen, the keyboard opened under the deck. The deck now steps aside while you type (character name, CD key fields, amounts in the game) and comes back afterwards.

## 0.1.4 (2026-11-05)
- Identify in the deck: tap an unidentified magic item, then *Identify* (with an identify scroll or a tome in the inventory or belt). Uses the game's own identify command, single player only.
- Horadric Cube in the deck: the *Cube* tab has *Open cube* and *Transmute*; put items in and take them out by touch as before. Uses the game's own cube commands.

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-04)
- Items by touch in the deck: tap an item to take it, tap a cell to put it there (inventory, equipment, stash and cube when open in the game, belt slots). Buttons: use, to belt, sell (shop open), drop, cancel. Uses the game's own pick-up and put-down commands, single player only.
- Button A also talks to NPCs and opens the stash (attack or take, as in D2Android). Taps into the world reach the game while the deck shows.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-04)
- CD key fields: every character masked at once, no full-screen keyboard text field in landscape, no suggestions or autofill. The key page is black in screenshots and recordings again (the AYN Thor's firmware dropped the usual protection flag; it is now set another way).

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-04)
- Skills on keys from the deck: tap a learnt skill, then left/right or F1–F8 (X, Y, R3, RB+…). Uses the game's own commands.
- Fresh installation without losing saved games (adb): the saves are parked, only the game is removed, and they come back afterwards.
- Quest titles in the deck in the game's language.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04)
- Deck on the lower screen: life and mana orbs, resistances, skills, effects, area and quests, inventory with item pictures, belt, equipment, stash, cube, mercenary, sort inventory, portal and potion.
- Fixed AYN Thor layout: RB second row, LT/RT potions, D-pad map and portal, A attack, B take.
- All pages in landscape; the installation shows its steps on the lower screen; CD keys masked.
